Tax Procedure Law Additional Article 14
For a MAP request to be evaluated, the MAP request must be submitted in the time and procedure provided in the DTA.
In a case that there is no time limit for a MAP request or there is a reference made to the time limits in the domestic law in the DTA, it is principal that the request must be
presented within three years from the first notification of the action resulting in taxation not in accordance with provisions of the DTA.
However the time period for a MAP request shall expire at the end of the period provided in the DTA, commencing from the date on which the notice has been communicated,
the tax has been accrued in the declaration submitted with reservation, and a deduction has been made in case of withholding; or else shall expire after the completion of 3
years if there is no such provided time limit.
