                  Business Taxation – CIT Law No. 5520 (1/12)
                              Subject and Base of CIT
 ¾Subject of Corporate Tax is the corporate profits derived by
 corporate taxpayers.
 ¾Corporate profits consist of all kinds of income elements that
 constitute the subject of income tax.
 ¾Corporate tax is calculated on the net corporate profit derived in
 a taxation period after deduction of expenses and allowances.

                   Free Zones Law No. 3218 (Cont’d)

  2003 that Turkish Free Trade Zones are not harmful in terms of
  ring-fenced taxation criteria.

                  Turkish International Ship Registration Code No.4490
                         (Amended by Article 3 of Law No. 5266)

  ¾ Earnings arising from operating and transferring of ships and

  exempted from duties, PIT and CIT.

  freight related to ships and yachts to be registered into Turkish

  Banking and Insurance Transaction Tax and Duties.

  ¾ Wages of staff working in ships and yachts registered into
  Turkish International Ship Registry, are exempted from income tax
  and duties.
