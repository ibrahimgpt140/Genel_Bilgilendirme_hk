3. TAXATION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES

 Proportional taxation is applied in the excise system for alcoholic
 beverages provided that the calculated tax is not less than the tax
 calculated according to the minimum specific tax amount.

  - Proportional Tax > Minimum Specific Tax
    Tax to be paid        Proportional Tax

  - Proportional Tax < Minimum Specific Tax
    Tax to be paid        Minimum Specific Tax

 For alcoholic beverages, minimum specific tax amounts are
 determined in 3 different categories. These are;

 ¾ 1st category       per alcohol degree in 1 liter of product
 ¾ 2nd category       1 liter of product
 ¾ 3rd category       per liter of pure alcohol contained.

4. EXAMPLES
 Taxation of Beer:
 Producer selling price of a bottle (50 cl.) of beer alcohol strength
 of which is 5, before tax is 2 €. Excise duty amount to be paid:
 ¾ Proportional tax rate: 63,3 %
 ¾ Minimum specific tax amount for per alcohol degree : 0,144 €
 ¾ Minimum specific tax amount for 1 liter of beer: 0,72 €
    (0,144 x 5= 0,72 € )
 ¾ Minimum specific tax amount for 1 bottle of beer: 0,36 €
    (0,50 x 0,72 € = 0,36 € )
 ¾ Tax calculated on proportional basis: 1,266 €
    (2 € x%63,3= 1,266 €)
      1,266 € > 0,36 €    excise duty amount to be paid is:1,266 €

8. TAX BASE (Article 11)
  Tax base of excise duty for alcoholic beverages; is the elements
    establishing base of value added tax, excluding excise duty.
  Excise duty should be indicated seperately on the sales invoice
    and other sales documents.
 Example:
 Selling price before tax of a gin subject to proportional excise duty
 at a rate of 275,6% is 10,00 €. It’s retail selling price is going to
 be calculated as follows:
 9 Value of a Good : 10,00 €
 9 Excise Duty          : 27,56 €
 9 Tax Base for VAT : 37,56 €
 9 VAT (18%)            : 6,76 €
 9 Total                : 44,32 €

9. AUTHORITY OF THE COUNCIL OF MINISTERS
   REGARDING TAX RATES (Article 12)
 The Council of Ministers has the authority to;
 ¾ decrease rates determined by Excise Duty Law down to zero
   for each products,
 ¾ increase them up to four fold,
 ¾ decrease minimum specific tax amounts determined by
   Excise Duty Law down to zero for each products,
 ¾ increase them up to two fold,
 ¾ determine different tax rates and minimum specific tax amounts
   within these limits according to the types, characteristics, price
   categories, alcohol strengths and alcohol content of the product
