   ¾   Identification of types of fraud in the direct taxation field, e.g.

    - non declaration of income earned abroad from work, savings
    or services
    - use by taxpayers of fictitious tax domicile, with the purpose of
    evading their tax obligations in their country of domicile for tax
    purposes
    - failure to submit tax returns and treatment as deductible costs
    unrelated to a business, such as stakeholders’ private expenses
    - under invoicing or over invoicing of goods and services

                          Framework (4)
       Report of the Council Ad Hoc Working Group
         of 22 May 2000 (FISC 67 – CRIMORG 183)
   ¾ Following this report, the Commission proposed
   amendments to Directive 77/799/EEC aiming at
   reinforcing Article 7 on limitations and at creating
   Article 8(3) on simultaneous controls (Directive
   2004/56)
   ¾ However, this is only a first step towards a better
   administrative cooperation at Community level as the
   most appropriate tool to fight against fiscal fraud at
   Community level.
